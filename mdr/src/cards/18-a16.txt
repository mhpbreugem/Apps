#AXVI|Annex XVI–XVII|Products without a medical purpose; correlation table|Annex XVI–XVII
Name the six groups of products without an intended medical purpose in Annex XVI.|(1) Contact lenses or other items introduced into or onto the eye; (2) products introduced into the body by surgically invasive means to modify the anatomy or fix body parts (except tattoos and piercings); (3) substances or items for facial or other dermal or mucous membrane filling by injection (except tattooing); (4) equipment to reduce, remove or destroy adipose tissue (liposuction, lipolysis, lipoplasty); (5) high-intensity electromagnetic radiation equipment (lasers, IPL) for skin resurfacing, tattoo or hair removal or other skin treatment; (6) brain stimulation equipment applying currents or fields that penetrate the cranium.|Annex XVI
Which implementing acts apply to Annex XVI products?|Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/2346 sets the common specifications for the Annex XVI groups (applicable from 22 June 2023, with transitional periods), and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/2347 reclassifies certain active products without an intended medical purpose.|Art. 1(2), 9
What is Annex XVII?|The correlation table between the provisions of Directives 90/385/EEC and 93/42/EEC and the MDR.|Annex XVII
