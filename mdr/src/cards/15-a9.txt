#AIX|Annex IX–XI|Conformity assessment procedures|Annex IX–XI
How is Annex IX structured?|Chapter I: quality management system (application, QMS assessment, surveillance assessment); Chapter II: assessment of the technical documentation (section 4) and specific additional procedures (section 5: CECP, medicinal substances, tissues or cells of human or animal origin, absorbed substances); Chapter III: administrative provisions (e.g. keeping documentation available).|Annex IX
How often must the notified body carry out surveillance audits and unannounced audits?|Surveillance audits and assessments at least once every 12 months, to check the manufacturer applies the approved QMS and PMS plan. Unannounced audits at random, at least once every five years, at the manufacturer's and where appropriate its suppliers' or subcontractors' premises, with sampling and testing of devices.|Annex IX 3.3–3.4
How does a notified body handle a device with an ancillary medicinal substance (Annex IX 5.2)?|It seeks a scientific opinion on the quality and safety of the substance, incl. the benefit-risk of incorporating it, from a national medicines authority or the EMA (the EMA is mandatory for substances derived from human blood or plasma or in the centralised procedure's scope). The opinion is due within 210 days of receiving valid documentation. The notified body must not issue the certificate if the opinion is unfavourable.|Annex IX 5.2
What is type-examination (Annex X)?|The procedure by which a notified body ascertains and certifies that a device, incl. its technical documentation, relevant life-cycle processes and a representative sample of production, meets the MDR. It results in an EU type-examination certificate and must be combined with Annex XI.|Annex X 1
What is the difference between Annex XI Part A and Part B?|Part A (production quality assurance): the manufacturer operates a QMS for production, audited by the notified body, ensuring devices conform to the type or technical documentation. Part B (product verification): the notified body examines and tests products (individually or by statistical batch sampling) to verify conformity, and issues an EU product verification certificate.|Annex XI
