#AII|Annex II–III|Technical documentation and PMS documentation|Annex II–III
What are the six main sections of the technical documentation in Annex II?|(1) Device description and specification, incl. variants and accessories; (2) information to be supplied by the manufacturer (labels, IFU); (3) design and manufacturing information; (4) general safety and performance requirements (GSPR checklist with methods, standards and CS); (5) benefit-risk analysis and risk management; (6) product verification and validation (pre-clinical and clinical data).|Annex II
What belongs in the device description section of the technical documentation (Annex II 1)?|Product or trade name and general description, intended purpose and users; Basic UDI-DI; intended patient population, indications, contra-indications and warnings; principles of operation and mode of action; qualification as a device; risk class and classification rule; novel features; accessories and other devices used in combination; variants and configurations; key components, materials and specifications; previous and similar generations of the device.|Annex II 1
What does product verification and validation in Annex II section 6 include?|Pre-clinical and clinical data: results of tests (engineering, laboratory, simulated use, animal), biocompatibility, physical, chemical and microbiological characterisation, electrical safety and EMC, software verification and validation, stability and shelf life, performance and safety; the clinical evaluation report, PMCF plan and PMCF evaluation report. Section 6.2 adds information for specific cases (medicinal substance, human or animal tissue, absorbed substances, CMR/ED, sterile, measuring function, connected devices).|Annex II 6
What does Annex III require for the PMS plan? Name five elements.|Collection of information (serious incidents, PSUR data, FSCAs, non-serious incidents and side-effects, trend reporting, literature, databases and registers, user, distributor and importer feedback and complaints, public information on similar devices); methods to assess data; indicators and thresholds for benefit-risk reassessment; complaint investigation tools; trend reporting methodology and observation period; communication with authorities, notified bodies, operators and users; procedures for Art. 83, 84 and 86; procedures for corrective action; traceability tools; and a PMCF plan or a justification why none is needed.|Annex III 1.1
What does section 1.2 of Annex III cover?|The periodic safety update report (Art. 86) and the post-market surveillance report (Art. 85) as part of the technical documentation on post-market surveillance.|Annex III 1.2
