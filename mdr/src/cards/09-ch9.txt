#IX|Chapter IX|Confidentiality, data protection, funding and penalties|Art. 109–113
Which three interests does the MDR's confidentiality rule protect?|(a) Personal data; (b) commercially confidential information and trade secrets, including intellectual property rights, unless disclosure is in the public interest; (c) the effective implementation of the MDR, in particular inspections, investigations and audits.|Art. 109(1)
Which data protection law applies to processing under the MDR?|Member States apply EU data protection law (Directive 95/46/EC, now the GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679); the Commission applies Regulation (EC) 45/2001 (now Regulation (EU) 2018/1725).|Art. 110
May Member States charge fees for MDR activities?|Yes, if the fees are set transparently and on cost-recovery principles. Member States inform the Commission and other Member States at least three months before adopting the fee structure and level, and make it publicly available on request.|Art. 111
Who pays for the joint assessment of notified bodies?|The Commission covers the costs of joint assessment activities and sets the scale and structure of recoverable fees by implementing acts.|Art. 112
What does the MDR require regarding penalties?|Member States lay down penalties for infringements of the MDR and ensure they are implemented. Penalties must be effective, proportionate and dissuasive. Member States notified their rules to the Commission by 25 February 2020.|Art. 113
