#AXII|Annex XII–XIII|Certificates and custom-made devices|Annex XII–XIII
Which types of certificates do notified bodies issue under the MDR?|EU quality management system certificates (Annex IX Ch. I), EU technical documentation assessment certificates (Annex IX Ch. II), EU type-examination certificates (Annex X), EU production quality assurance certificates (Annex XI Part A) and EU product verification certificates (Annex XI Part B).|Annex XII
Name six items a notified body certificate must contain (Annex XII).|Notified body name, address and number; manufacturer (and AR) name and address and SRN; unique certificate number; dates of issue and expiry; data to identify the devices, incl. Basic UDI-DI; reference to any previous certificate; reference to the MDR and Annex; examinations and tests performed; surveillance information; conclusions of the assessment; conditions or limitations; legally binding signature.|Annex XII Ch. II
What must the statement for a custom-made device contain (Annex XIII)?|Manufacturer name and address and all manufacturing sites; AR if applicable; data identifying the device; that it is for the exclusive use of a particular patient or user (name, acronym or code); the prescriber's name and, where applicable, the health institution; the specific characteristics per the prescription; that it conforms to the Annex I GSPR, or which GSPR are not fully met and why; and whether it contains a medicinal substance or tissues or cells of human or animal origin. It is made available to the patient or user.|Annex XIII 1
How long must custom-made device documentation be kept?|At least 10 years after the device has been placed on the market; 15 years for implantable devices.|Annex XIII 4
