#III|Chapter III|UDI, registration, SSCP and Eudamed|Art. 25–34
Which supply-chain parties must an economic operator be able to identify (Art. 25)?|For the retention period of Art. 10(8): (a) any economic operator to whom it has directly supplied a device; (b) any economic operator who has directly supplied it with a device; (c) any health institution or healthcare professional to whom it has directly supplied a device.|Art. 25(2)
Which medical device nomenclature does the Commission provide under the MDR?|The European Medical Device Nomenclature (EMDN), made available free of charge, based on the Italian CND classification.|Art. 26
What are the two parts of a UDI?|The UDI-DI (device identifier), specific to a manufacturer and a device model, giving access to the Eudamed information; and the UDI-PI (production identifier), identifying the unit of production, such as serial number, lot number, software identification, manufacturing date and/or expiry date.|Art. 27(1); Annex VI Part C
What is the Basic UDI-DI?|The primary identifier of a device model, assigned at the level of the device unit of use. It is the main key in the UDI database and is referenced in certificates, EU declarations of conformity, technical documentation and the SSCP. It does not appear on the label.|Art. 27(6), 29; Annex VI Part C
Which UDI issuing entities has the Commission designated?|GS1, HIBCC, ICCBBA and IFA GmbH (Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/939).|Art. 27(2)
What is a UDI carrier and where must it be placed?|The means of conveying the UDI by automatic identification and data capture (AIDC: e.g. linear or 2D bar code, RFID) and its human readable interpretation (HRI). It goes on the label of the device and on all higher levels of packaging; shipping containers are excluded. Reusable devices need the UDI carrier on the device itself (direct marking).|Art. 27(4); Annex VI Part C
Which UDIs must economic operators and health institutions store?|Economic operators must store, preferably electronically, the UDIs of class III implantable devices they supplied or were supplied with (and other devices the Commission specifies). Health institutions must do the same for class III implantable devices; Member States encourage, and may require, this for other devices.|Art. 27(8)–(9)
From when must the UDI carrier be on labels, per risk class?|Class III and implantable devices: 26 May 2021. Class IIa and IIb: 26 May 2023. Class I: 26 May 2025. Direct marking of reusable devices follows two years later for each class (2023, 2025, 2027).|Art. 123(3)(f)–(g)
What must a manufacturer do before placing a device (other than custom-made) on the market under Art. 29?|Assign a Basic UDI-DI and provide it with the other core data elements (Annex VI Part B) to the UDI database. For devices needing a notified body, the Basic UDI-DI must be assigned before applying to the notified body.|Art. 29(1)–(2)
What is the SRN and how is it obtained?|The Single Registration Number, issued by the competent authority after a manufacturer, authorised representative or importer registers in the Eudamed actor registration system. It is used in applications to notified bodies and in Eudamed for UDI obligations.|Art. 31
Which economic operators must register as actors in Eudamed? Do distributors?|Manufacturers, authorised representatives and importers. Distributors have no actor registration obligation under the MDR (national rules may differ).|Art. 30–31
What registration deadlines apply to importers and to changes of actor data?|Within two weeks of placing a device on the market, importers verify that the manufacturer or AR registered the device information and add their own details. Economic operators update their data within one week of any change, and confirm its accuracy one year after submission and every second year thereafter.|Art. 30(3), 31(4)–(5)
Which devices need a summary of safety and clinical performance (SSCP)?|Implantable devices and class III devices, other than custom-made or investigational devices. The draft is submitted to and validated by the notified body, and the SSCP is made public through Eudamed.|Art. 32(1)
Name six items the SSCP must contain.|Device and manufacturer identification (Basic UDI-DI, SRN); intended purpose, indications, contraindications and target populations; device description incl. previous generations, variants, accessories and combined devices; possible diagnostic or therapeutic alternatives; harmonised standards and CS applied; summary of the clinical evaluation and PMCF; suggested user profile and training; residual risks, undesirable effects, warnings and precautions.|Art. 32(2)
How often must the SSCP be updated?|For class III and implantable devices, the PMCF evaluation report and, if indicated, the SSCP are updated at least annually with PMCF and PMS data.|Art. 61(11)
Which electronic systems make up Eudamed?|Registration of devices; the UDI database; registration of economic operators (actors); notified bodies and certificates; clinical investigations; vigilance and post-market surveillance; market surveillance.|Art. 33(2)
How did Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 change the roll-out of Eudamed?|It allows a gradual roll-out: each Eudamed module becomes mandatory on its own once the Commission has verified its functionality and published a notice in the Official Journal, with use becoming mandatory about six months after that notice, instead of waiting for the whole system to be functional.|Art. 34, 123(3)(d) as amended
