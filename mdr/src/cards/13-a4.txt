#AIV|Annex IV–VI|Declaration of conformity, CE marking, registration and UDI|Annex IV–VI
Name six elements of the EU declaration of conformity (Annex IV).|Manufacturer (and AR) name, registered trade name or mark, SRN and address; statement that it is issued under the manufacturer's sole responsibility; Basic UDI-DI; product and trade name, product code or other reference and intended purpose; risk class; statement of conformity with the MDR and other relevant Union legislation; references to common specifications used; notified body name and number, procedure and certificate(s); additional information; place and date of issue, signatory's name and function, signature.|Annex IV
What are the design rules for the CE marking (Annex V)?|The initials "CE" in the prescribed form; if reduced or enlarged, the proportions of the graduated drawing must be respected; the components must have substantially the same vertical dimension, at least 5 mm (this minimum may be waived for small-scale devices).|Annex V
How is Annex VI structured?|Part A: information submitted when registering devices and economic operators (Art. 29(4), 31); Part B: core data elements for the UDI database with the UDI-DI (Art. 28, 29); Part C: the UDI system (definitions, general requirements, UDI-DI, UDI-PI, UDI carrier, specific rules e.g. for software and systems).|Annex VI
When does a change require a new UDI-DI?|Whenever a change could lead to misidentification of the device or ambiguity in its traceability, in particular a change of: name or trade name, version or model, labelled as single use, packaged sterile, need for sterilisation before use, quantity of devices in a package, critical warnings or contra-indications (e.g. latex or DEHP).|Annex VI Part C 3.9
When does software need a new UDI-DI rather than a new UDI-PI?|A new UDI-DI is needed for modifications that change the original performance, the safety or intended use, or the interpretation of data (e.g. new or modified algorithms, database structures, operating platform, architecture, user interfaces or interoperability channels). Minor revisions (bug fixes, usability enhancements not for safety, security patches, operating efficiency) need only a new UDI-PI.|Annex VI Part C 6.5
